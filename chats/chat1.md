I've built the landing page: **Salena-Jos Landing Page.dc.html**. It's designed for phones first and widens into two columns on desktop. A final check is running in the background.

**What's on the page**
- **Opening screen:** their SJ crown logo, taken from the shopfront photo. A soft light sweeps across it every few seconds, like the silver window lettering. Their sign line "For all your beauty needs" is the headline, with Book and Call buttons and a slow-scrolling strip of the treatments.
- **Treatments:** the six from the A-board, each opening to a short description, a "Book this" button and a space for a photo.
- **Training Academy:** a dark band about Salena-Jo's 20+ years as a qualified beauty lecturer.
- **Visit:** the storefront photo, the address, a live "open now / closed" message and the opening hours.
- **Booking:** three steps (treatment, then day and time, then your details), with checks on the form, a confirmation screen and an "Add to calendar" button. Ending with "we'll text to confirm" keeps it honest, because nothing actually gets sent yet.
- **Footer:** contact details, plus a floating Call / Book bar on phones.
- **Motion:** sections fade and rise as you scroll, photos drift slightly, the menu slides open full-screen, and the booking card resizes smoothly between steps. All motion switches off for visitors whose phones are set to reduce motion.
- **Settings you can switch:** online booking on/off, the floating bar on/off, and the logo light sweep on/off.

**Please check**
- **Opening hours:** I took these from a public directory, which only listed Wednesday to Saturday. Monday, Tuesday and Sunday show as closed for now.
- **Address and email:** 64 Ashby Road, LE11 3AE and salenajo7@aol.com also come from public listings.
- **Booking:** to take real bookings it needs connecting to a booking service such as Fresha or Treatwell, or to email or SMS.
- **Hair:** it's on their shop sign but not on the A-board, so it appears only in the strapline. Tell me if it should be bookable.
- **Photos:** each treatment has an empty photo space you can drag pictures straight into, and the storefront photo can be swapped the same way. Their own work will lift the design the most.
